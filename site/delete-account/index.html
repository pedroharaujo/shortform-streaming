Delete your Stovio account

Last updated: 26 September 2026

This page explains how to delete your account in the Stovio Android app, operated by Pedro Henrique Araujo Pinto, and what happens to your data.

Option 1: in the app

  1. Open Stovio and sign in.
  2. Open Account.
  3. Open Account management.
  4. Tap Delete account.
  5. Confirm with your password, or with the same Google account you use to sign in.

Your Stovio data is deleted straight away and you are signed out. Your sign-in account is normally removed at the same time; if that step is delayed, we complete it as soon as possible.

Option 2: without the app

  1. Email pharaujo1094@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Stovio account.
  2. Send it from the email address you use to sign in to Stovio (for Google sign-in, that Google account's address).
  3. We may reply to confirm the account is yours. We never ask for your password, verification codes or payment card details.

We delete the account within one month of confirming the request, and email you when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your Stovio sign-in account (email/password or Google sign-in link).
  • Your profile, language, country and privacy choices.
  • Your watch history, playback positions and settings such as automatic unlocking.
  • Your access to episodes you unlocked, and any remaining coins.

Deletion cannot be undone. Remaining coins and unlocked episodes cannot be recovered or moved to another account.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Coin purchase and spending records, separated from your sign-in account: kept for as long as needed to meet legal accounting and tax obligations and to handle refunds, chargebacks and disputes, then deleted.
  • A record that the deletion happened, without your name or email: up to three years, to prove the request was completed.
  • Support emails: up to 12 months after your request is closed.
  • Backups: deleted data can remain in backups for up to 35 days before they expire.

Google Play keeps its own record of your purchases under Google's privacy policy. RevenueCat, which verifies purchases for us, holds purchase records linked to a random identifier rather than your name or email.
